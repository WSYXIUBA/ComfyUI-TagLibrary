# 姿势动作

arm support(手臂支撑), leaning forward(前倾), leaning back(后仰), head tilt(歪头), head rest(托腮), chin rest(托下巴)
neck stretch(活动脖子), self hug(自我拥抱), leaning on wall(倚墙), leaning on table(倚桌), looking out window(望向窗外), chin on hand(托腮)
head in hands(双手抱头), resting chin on knees(下巴搁膝), leaning on elbow(肘撑), head against wall(头靠墙), head tilted back(头后仰), neck exposed(露出脖子)
cheek rest(托脸), dramatic lean(戏剧性前倾), back arch(塌腰挺胸), chest forward(挺胸), shoulders back(肩膀后展), slouching(没精打采驼背)
hunching(弓背)
